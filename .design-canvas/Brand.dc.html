The mark, and what it is arguing

The mark

A question mark whose dot is a check — “정확한지는 모르겠지만, 아무튼 통했다” in one glyph. The hook is the doubt; the tick underneath is the outcome. It is drawn rather than typeset because no face puts a tick there, and it stays open at the bottom: the question is never actually resolved, only the conversation is.

The wordmark

The name is fifteen unspaced Hangul syllables. Set as one run with break-all it wrapped mid-syllable and the brand's biggest asset arrived looking like a layout bug. The clause break fixes the wrap and stages the joke: the setup reads small and hedging, the punchline reads big and shrugging.

Before · break-all 아무튼서로알아들었으면된거아닌가요 Wraps inside a syllable. Reads as broken rendering.
After · clause break 아무튼서로알아들었으면 된거아닌가요 Holds its shape down to 320px. 아서알? is the compact form.

The symbol

One bubble over-explains; the other says “ㅇㅇ”. The check sits on the overlap — the only saturated thing in the mark, so the eye lands on the agreement rather than on either speaker. Communication is not equal effort or matched grammar. It is only the overlap.

Brand devices — and where they are banned

Allowed on hero, onboarding, empty, success and brand illustration. Never inside a live reading area or a form somebody is trying to complete. If a device makes a task harder it comes out — the concept survives that fine.
